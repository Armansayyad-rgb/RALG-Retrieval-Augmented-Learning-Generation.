HVAC Maintenance Controller Operation Manual
============================================

1. SAFETY PRECAUTIONS
---------------------
- Always perform lockout-tagout on electrical power before servicing HVAC units.
- Verify zero electrical potential at disconnect before removing panels.
- Recover refrigerant per EPA regulations before opening refrigerant circuit.
- Wear appropriate PPE when working with refrigerants, electrical components, and moving parts.
- Never bypass safety controls or temperature limiters.

2. NORMAL OPERATION
--------------------
- System provides heating when heating mode is selected and thermostat calls for heat.
- System provides cooling when cooling mode is selected and thermostat calls for cool.
- Normal supply air temperature range: 55-75°F cooling, 90-120°F heating.
- Fan operates at variable speed: 0-100% depending on demand.
- Air filter should be checked monthly and replaced when dirty.

3. STARTUP SEQUENCE (HEATING)
------------------------------
a. Confirm thermostat is set to HEAT mode and temperature is above current room temperature.
b. Verify gas supply is on (for gas-fired units) or electrical connections are secure (for electric units).
c. Inspect air filter - replace if dirty or restricted.
d. Check that all access panels are secure and guards are in place.
e. Set thermostat to desired temperature.
f. System should initiate ignition sequence after 30-second pre-purge.
g. Verify flame is established and burners are operating normally.
h. Monitor supply air temperature rises to normal range within 5 minutes.
i. Fan will begin blowing heated air once heat exchanger reaches safe temperature.

4. STARTUP SEQUENCE (COOLING)
-------------------------------
a. Confirm thermostat is set to COOL mode and temperature is below current room temperature.
b. Verify electrical connections are secure and power is supplied to compressor.
c. Inspect air filter - replace if dirty or restricted.
d. Check that all access panels are secure and guards are in place.
e. Set thermostat to desired temperature.
f. Compressor should start after thermostat call for cool.
g. Verify condenser fan is operating.
h. Check that supply air temperature drops to normal range within 5 minutes.
i. System will cycle on and off to maintain setpoint.

5. INSPECTION PHASE
--------------------
- Check air filter condition and airflow - replace if dirty.
- Inspect supply and return registers for obstructions.
- Check thermostat calibration and operation.
- Inspect evaporator coil for frost or ice formation (cooling mode).
- Inspect heat exchanger for cracks or damage (heating mode).
- Verify refrigerant lines for leaks (trace with detector solution).
- Check blower motor and fan for unusual noise or vibration.
- Inspect ductwork for leaks, loose connections, or damage.
- Verify safety controls are functional (limit switches, pressure controls).

6. TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------

PROBLEM: System will not start (heating)
- Cause: Thermostat not calling for heat - check settings.
- Cause: No gas supply (gas units) - verify gas valve is open.
- Cause: Electrical fault - check voltage at disconnect, fuses, circuit breaker.
- Cause: Flame failure - reset after safety lockout, inspect burner.
- Cause: Limit switch tripped - reset after addressing root cause.

PROBLEM: System will not start (cooling)
- Cause: Thermostat not calling for cool - check settings.
- Cause: Compressor protection trip - reset thermal overload.
- Cause: Electrical fault - check voltage, fuses, circuit breaker.
- Cause: Refrigerant low - system may have leak, require recharge.
- Cause: High-pressure switch tripped - reset after addressing cause.

PROBLEM: Insufficient heating
- Cause: Dirty air filter - restrict airflow, replace filter.
- Cause: Thermostat not calibrated - recalibrate or replace.
- Cause: Gas pressure too low - adjust gas valve, check supply.
- Cause: Heat exchanger restricted - clean or replace.
- Cause: Duct leaks - seal ductwork, insulate as needed.

PROBLEM: Insufficient cooling
- Cause: Dirty air filter - restrict airflow, replace filter.
- Cause: Dirty evaporator coil - clean coil.
- Cause: Dirty condenser coil - clean outdoor unit coil.
- Cause: Refrigerant low - system has leak, locate and repair, recharge.
- Cause: High outdoor temperature - system may not meet load demand.
- Cause: Thermostat not calibrated - recalibrate or replace.

PROBLEM: Short cycling (frequent on/off)
- Cause: Oversized system for space - may require load calculation.
- Cause: Dirty filter - restrict airflow, replace filter.
- Cause: Thermostat located near heat source - relocate thermostat.
- Cause: Low refrigerant - system has leak, locate and repair, recharge.
- Cause: High static pressure in ductwork - redesign duct system.

PROBLEM: compressor will not start
- Cause: Electrical fault - check voltage, fuses, circuit breaker.
- Cause: compressor protection trip - reset thermal overload after cooling.
- Cause: capacitor failure - replace capacitor.
- Cause: contactor failure - replace contactor.
- Cause: thermostat not calling - check settings and wiring.

7. REFRIGERANT SERVICE
------------------------
- Recover refrigerant: Use certified recovery machine per EPA Section 608.
- Leak detection: Electronic leak detector or trace with Nitrogen soap solution.
- Recharge: Add only specified refrigerant type and amount per nameplate.
- Never mix refrigerant types.
- Document all refrigerant additions and removals per regulatory requirements.

8. MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
------------------------
- Daily: Check thermostat operation, listen for unusual noises, verify airflow.
- Every 250 hours: Check air filter, inspect visual condition of unit.
- Every 500 hours: Clean or replace air filter, inspect evaporator/condenser coils.
- Every 1000 hours: Check refrigerant pressure, test safety controls, lubricate motors.
- Every 2000 hours: Clean burners (gas units), inspect heat exchanger, check belt tension.
- Annual: Full combustion analysis (gas units), refrigerant charge verification, complete safety inspection.

9. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
------------------------
- Refrigerant leak: Evacuate area, ventilate, call qualified HVAC technician.
- Electrical fire: Cut power at disconnect, call fire department, do not use water on electrical.
- Gas leak (gas units): Evacuate area, ventilate, do not operate electrical switches, call gas company.

10. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
----------------------------
- Model: HVAC-400 Maintenance Controller
- Capacity: 2-ton to 20-ton variable capacity
- Voltage: 208/240V single-phase or 208/3-phase
- Refrigerant: R-410A (or approved alternate)
- Airflow range: 400-2000 CFM
- Temperature range: -10°F to 120°F ambient
- Protection class: NEMA 1 (indoor), NEMA 3R (outdoor units)
- Connection: Standard HVAC plug-in connections
- Motor: ECM variable-speed blower motor
- Compressor: Scroll type, 2-20 HP
