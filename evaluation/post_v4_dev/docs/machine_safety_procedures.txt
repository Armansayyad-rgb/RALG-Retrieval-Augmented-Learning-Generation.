Fictional Machine Safety Procedures
=====================================

1. GENERAL SAFETY PRINCIPLES
-----------------------------
- Lockout-tagout (LOTO) must be performed before any maintenance activity.
- Never bypass or disable safety interlocks or emergency stop controls.
- All personnel must be trained on hazards before operating equipment.
- Safety procedures apply to all equipment types regardless of size or capacity.
- Emergency procedures must be posted at each workstation.

2. LOCKOUT-TAGOUT (LOTO) PROCEDURE
-------------------------------------
STEP 1: PREPARATION
- Identify all energy sources (electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, mechanical, thermal).
- Verify the authorized personnel and required equipment for the LOTO.
- Review the equipment-specific LOTO procedure before beginning.

STEP 2: SHUTDOWN
- Stop the equipment using the normal shutdown procedure.
- Allow moving parts to come to complete stop.
- Disconnect process material from equipment.

STEP 3: ISOLATION
- Identify all energy isolation points (disconnect switches, valves, breakers).
- Operate all isolation devices to isolate equipment from energy sources.
- Verify isolation by attempting normal startup (equipment should not start).

STEP 4: APPLICATION OF LOCKOUT DEVICES
- Apply personal lockout device to each isolated energy point.
- Each technician applies their own lock with personal key/code.
- Verify each lock is properly seated and secured.

STEP 5: APPLICATION OF TAGS
- Apply personal tag with technician name, date, and reason.
- Tags must be visible and legible.
- Never remove another person's lock or tag without authorization.

STEP 6: RELEASE OF STORED ENERGY
- Release residual mechanical energy (springs, counterweights, gravity).
- Drain hydraulic/pneumatic systems to zero pressure.
- Allow capacitors to discharge (verify with meter).
- Open bleed valves to relieve pneumatic/hydraulic pressure.

STEP 7: VERIFICATION
- Attempt normal equipment startup to verify isolation.
- Verify all energy sources are zero or isolated.
- Confirm no one is near the equipment before proceeding.

3. EMERGENCY STOP (E-STOP) PROCEDURE
---------------------------------------
- Press E-STOP button immediately when hazard detected.
- E-STOP must cut power to all moving functions immediately.
- After E-STOP activation, confirm motion has ceased before approaching.
- E-STOP reset must only be performed after hazard is resolved.
- Only the person who activated E-STOP (or authorized rescue personnel) should reset.

4. MACHINE-SPECIFIC SAFETY PROCEDURES
---------------------------------------
PROCEDURE A: PUMP MAINTENANCE
- Perform LOTO on pump motor electrical supply.
- Verify zero voltage at disconnect before removing panels.
- Drain any fluid from pump housing before opening.
- Support any suspended components before removal.

PROCEDURE B: CONVEYOR MAINTENANCE
- Perform LOTO on conveyor drive electrical supply.
- Verify zero voltage at disconnect before entering conveyor zone.
- Secure conveyor belt to prevent accidental movement.
- Lock out all drive motors and associated control circuits.

PROCEDURE C: HVAC UNIT MAINTENANCE
- Perform LOTO on electrical supply and gas line (if gas-fired).
- Recover refrigerant per EPA regulations before opening circuit.
- Verify zero electrical potential and zero gas pressure.
- Support access panels before removal.

PROCEDURE D: BATTERY SYSTEM MAINTENANCE
- Perform LOTO on charger and load circuits.
- Verify zero voltage at battery terminals.
- Remove conductive jewelry, use insulated tools.
- Ensure adequate ventilation before beginning work.

5. CONFINED SPACE ENTRY
-------------------------
- Test atmosphere for oxygen deficiency and toxic gases.
- Verify forced ventilation is operating.
- Attend observer must be stationed outside confined space.
- Retrieval equipment must be available and functional.
- Permit system must be completed before entry.

6. HOT WORK PERMIT
--------------------
- Fire watch must be posted during and 30 minutes after hot work.
- Fire extinguishing equipment must be immediately available.
- Combustible materials must be removed from work area or protected.
- Hot work permit must be completed and authorized before beginning.

7. CHEMICAL HANDLING
--------------------
- Review Safety Data Sheet (SDS) before handling any chemical.
- Use appropriate PPE per SDS recommendations.
- Store chemicals in designated areas, properly labeled.
- In case of spill, follow spill containment procedure.
- Dispose of chemical waste per regulatory requirements.

8. PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
----------------------------------------
- Safety glasses with side shields must be worn at all times in work areas.
- Steel-toed boots required where heavy objects may fall.
- Hearing protection where noise levels exceed 85 dB(A).
- Gloves appropriate for hazard (chemical, thermal, electrical).
- Hard hats where overhead hazards exist.
- Respiratory protection where airborne contaminants exceed PEL.

9. INCIDENT REPORTING
----------------------
- All incidents, near-misses, and safety violations must be reported within 24 hours.
- Supervisor must investigate all incidents within 48 hours.
- Root cause analysis required for any recordable incident.
- Corrective actions must be implemented and verified.
- Incident reports must be retained for minimum 3 years.

10. SAFETY TRAINING REQUIREMENTS
----------------------------------
- Initial safety training before first assignment.
- Annual refresher training on all safety procedures.
- Task-specific training before performing new procedures.
- Emergency drill participation quarterly.
- LOTO certification renewed annually.
- Emergency response training semi-annually.
